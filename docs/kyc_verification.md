# Identity Verification (KYC / AML) Guidelines

## 1. Verification Tiers
To comply with global Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations, accounts operate under two verification tiers:

### Tier 1: Basic Identity Verification
- **Required Documents**: Government-issued national ID card, passport, or driver's license, alongside a real-time liveness selfie check.
- **Review SLA**: Automated verification completes within **15 minutes**.
- **Privileges**: Enables deposits and standard platform access.

### Tier 2: Enhanced Verification & Proof of Address
- **Required Documents**: A utility bill, bank statement, or council tax statement issued within the last **90 days (3 months)** with the user's full name and residential address clearly visible.
- **Review SLA**: Manual compliance review requires **24 to 48 business hours**.
- **Privileges**: Unlocks higher withdrawal limits and fiat wire transfer operations.

## 2. Strict Compliance and Non-Bypass Policy
- All KYC verification workflows are governed by automated risk engines and certified compliance officers.
- **Customer support agents have no technical or procedural ability to manually bypass, expedite, or override identity verification under any circumstances.**
- This non-bypass policy applies universally to all users, including VIP accounts, institutional clients, and high-net-worth individuals. No expedited verification fees or fast-track bypass services exist.
