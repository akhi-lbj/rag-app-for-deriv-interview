# Payment Processing and Withdrawal Timelines

## 1. Supported Payment Methods
The platform supports deposits and withdrawals across three primary rails:
- Credit and Debit Cards (Visa / Mastercard)
- Bank Wire Transfers (SEPA, SWIFT, and ACH)
- Digital Assets / Cryptocurrency (USDC, USDT on Ethereum and Tron networks)

## 2. Withdrawal Processing Windows & SLAs
- **Fiat Wire Transfers**: Processed within **1 to 3 business days** following compliance approval. Banks may require an additional 24-48 hours depending on intermediary routing.
- **Credit/Debit Card Refunds/Payouts**: Typically settle within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Cryptocurrency Withdrawals**: Automated and broadcast to the blockchain within **30 minutes** after requisite network security confirmations. Note: blockchain network fees apply.

## 3. Minimum Withdrawal Amounts
- Wire Transfers: Minimum withdrawal is **$50 USD** (or currency equivalent).
- Digital Assets (USDC/USDT): Minimum withdrawal is **$10 USD**.

## 4. Withdrawal Security Holds
- For user protection against account compromise, modifying security credentials (such as password reset, 2FA re-enrollment, or email update) imposes an automated **24-hour security hold** on all withdrawal requests.
- Crypto withdrawals to newly whitelisted wallet addresses require a 6-hour cooling-off period.
